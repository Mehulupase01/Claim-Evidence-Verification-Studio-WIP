Studio WIP Quarterly Operations Note

The second quarter closed with revenue 27 percent above the same quarter last year. The increase came primarily from three retained client programmes and two new production engagements.

Operating costs rose 9 percent during the quarter. The team did not open a second office, and headcount remained at 18 people through the reporting date.

The studio plans to review hiring needs again in October. No decision has been made about expanding into a new region.
